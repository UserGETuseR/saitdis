HERO VIDEO — drop your file here
=================================

Put your hero background video in THIS folder as:

    hero.mp4      (required — MP4 / H.264 + AAC works everywhere)
    hero.webm     (optional — smaller, modern browsers)

The site's hero (top of the page) will automatically use video/hero.mp4 as a
looping, muted, autoplaying background. If the file is missing or fails to load,
the site falls back to a photo background — nothing breaks.

Recommended: short (8–20s) seamless loop, 1920x1080 (or 1080x1920 for mobile
feel), muted, H.264. Keep it under ~10–15 MB so it loads fast.

WHERE TO GET FREE, COMMERCIALLY-LICENSED VIDEO
----------------------------------------------
  * Pexels Video   — https://www.pexels.com/videos/
  * Mixkit         — https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/
  * Coverr         — https://coverr.co/

These allow free commercial use (always double-check the specific license).

DO NOT use downloads from Pinterest / Klickpin / random re-uploads — those are
NOT licensed for commercial use and may infringe copyright.

After dropping hero.mp4 here, just double-click START.bat to view the site.
